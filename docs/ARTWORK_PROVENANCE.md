# Artwork provenance

The publisher answered the artwork review on 2026-11-11: the old launcher icon
had an unknown origin and needed replacement; the other older artwork was
confirmed free of third-party rights, with MIT permitted after icon replacement.

The old `drawable/ic_launcher_colibri.jpg` has been removed from current source
and release inputs. A new hummingbird mark was independently generated with the
built-in Image Generation tool, without supplying the old image as a reference.
Its source is `artwork/source/launcher-hummingbird.png`; the identical resource
is `app/src/main/res/drawable-nodpi/ic_launcher_colibri.png`.

The November 3 generated originals for both lagoon backgrounds (including
flowers) and the hummingbird body/wing were located and visually checked against
the application. The generated files and prompts/provenance are now included
in [artwork/README.md](../artwork/README.md) and `artwork/source/`.

Project artwork is distributed under MIT to the extent rights apply; no
exclusive rights over generated subject matter are claimed. The publisher's
confirmation covers legacy project artwork such as `assets/surfboard.svg`.
Compose scene drawing and animation are project code under MIT. Third-party
source-tree materials retain their upstream license notices.
